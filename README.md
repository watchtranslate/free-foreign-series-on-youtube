## FAQ

**Are these episodes free to watch?**

Yes. Every link goes to the network's or the show's own official YouTube channel, where the episodes are posted for free.

**My language isn't listed for a series. Can I still watch it with subtitles?**

Yes. The links point to pages written in those languages, but WatchTranslate translates into many more: open any episode and pick your language.
